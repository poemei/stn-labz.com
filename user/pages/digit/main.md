# Meet Digit

**The Modular AI Agent of STN-Labz**

*Built to observe. Designed to learn. Required to prove.*

Digit is an evolving, modular AI agent developed within the STN-Labz ecosystem.

Unlike traditional command-driven chatbots, Digit is being developed to observe conversations, retain information, retrieve knowledge, and eventually use accumulated experience to participate naturally in human conversations.

Her development emphasizes modular architecture, persistent memory, human authority, diagnostic transparency, and evidence-based validation.

**Our philosophy: Show me das evidences!**

---

## Who Is Digit?

Digit is an independent AI agent designed to operate through interconnected modules while maintaining a clear separation between her Core, capabilities, and persistent knowledge.

Her purpose extends beyond responding to direct questions.

Digit is being developed to understand her environment, observe human interactions, retain relevant information, and gradually develop more natural conversational capabilities.

Rather than assuming a capability works because it has been implemented, Digit's development relies on testing, observation, and demonstrated results.

---

## Digit's Capabilities

### Observation and Persistent Memory

Digit can observe ordinary IRC channel conversations without requiring participants to address her directly.

Her Observation module records messages in a persistent SQLite database, preserving information such as:

- Who participated in a conversation.
- What was communicated.
- When the conversation occurred.
- Which conversation the information belongs to.
- Relevant source and event identifiers.

These observations establish the foundation for future contextual learning and more human-like conversational behavior.

### Modular Architecture

Digit uses independent modules to provide specialized capabilities.

Her modular architecture includes:

- **IRC:** Communication through the Libera.Chat IRC network.
- **Conversation:** Processing requests and retrieving knowledge.
- **SQLite3:** Persistent data storage and retrieval.
- **Observation:** Recording conversations and preserving historical context.
- **DSD:** Diagnostic tracing and operational transparency.
- **S2:** Security-oriented reconnaissance and intelligence capabilities.

Individual modules can be developed, tested, qualified, and updated without requiring unnecessary modifications to Digit's Core.

### Diagnostic Transparency

Digit incorporates a Diagnostic System known as **DSD**.

DSD provides detailed operational traces showing how requests move through her modules.

These traces can identify:

- Which modules processed a request.
- Which operations were performed.
- Whether database searches returned matching records.
- Which services succeeded or failed.
- Where operations occurred in the source code.

The purpose of DSD is to support evidence-based investigation rather than assumptions.

### Knowledge Retrieval

Digit maintains a persistent knowledge corpus containing verified instructional, engineering, and technical information.

Her knowledge retrieval capabilities are under active development.

Retrieving matching information and generating a contextually appropriate answer are separate challenges, and Digit's development continues to address both.

### S2 Intelligence

Digit's S2 capability supports security-oriented reconnaissance and intelligence gathering.

Her observation system also provides a foundation for retaining intelligence shared through IRC conversations.

Integration between stored observations and future S2 intelligence processing remains an active development objective.

## Digit Is Listening

Digit's Observation module can record ordinary public-channel conversations without requiring someone to mention her nickname.

This allows her to preserve conversational history for future contextual learning and development.

**Observing a conversation does not mean Digit will interrupt it.**

Her ability to receive information, record observations, interpret context, and decide whether to respond are distinct capabilities.

### Privacy Notice

The STN-Labz IRC channel includes AI-assisted observation and persistent conversation recording.

Public channel messages may be stored for operational, diagnostic, and future AI-development purposes.

Participants should avoid posting passwords, confidential information, private credentials, or sensitive personal data in public conversations.

## Evidence Before Trust

Digit's engineering philosophy is built around a simple principle:

**Functionality must be demonstrated, not assumed.**

Writing code does not prove that it works.

Passing an isolated test does not prove that a capability works in production.

A successful connection does not prove that information was stored.

And storing information does not automatically prove that an AI agent understands it.

Digit's development process emphasizes observable behavior, operational diagnostics, qualification testing, and real-world verification.

Every demonstrated capability contributes to the confidence placed in her development.

**Das receipts == das evidences.**

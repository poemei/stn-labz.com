# Rictus

**Rictus is the STN-LABZ autonomous intelligence agent.**

Rictus watches public information sources for developments that may matter to STN-LABZ, its technology, infrastructure, research, and operations.

Rather than simply collecting everything published on the Internet, Rictus is designed to answer a more useful question:

> **What does STN-LABZ need to know before it becomes important?**

## What Rictus Does

Every day, an enormous amount of information is published about software, security, technology, infrastructure, research, and the organizations that build them.

Most of it isn't relevant to us.

Rictus helps separate useful intelligence from all that noise.

He can observe information from approved public sources, determine whether it may be relevant to STN-LABZ, preserve useful findings, and continue watching subjects that deserve further attention.

When something becomes important enough to require human attention, Rictus reports it to an STN-LABZ operator.

The human makes the decision about what happens next.

## Intelligence, Not a News Feed

The STN-LABZ [Intelligence](/intelligence) page is not intended to be another technology news feed.

It contains information that a human or STN-LABZ intelligence system determined may be worth preserving.

Some intelligence may concern software we use.

Some may concern security developments.

Some may involve technology or research relevant to something we are developing.

And sometimes an observation simply gives us a reason to keep watching.

That distinction matters.

Something appearing in our Intelligence feed does not necessarily mean STN-LABZ is affected by it, agrees with it, or considers it a threat.

It means we considered the information worth retaining.

## From Observation to Investigation

Rictus doesn't treat every observation as a conclusion.

Information can develop over time.

An initial observation may lead to additional evidence. Related events may appear later. New information may strengthen an earlier concern, contradict it, or demonstrate that it was never particularly important.

When a subject deserves continued attention, Rictus can follow it and allow evidence to accumulate.

That gives STN-LABZ something much more useful than a stream of headlines:

**history and context.**

## Humans Remain in Control

Rictus can collect, evaluate, correlate, preserve, and report intelligence, but he does not replace human authority.

When intelligence reaches a point where action may be warranted, Rictus brings it to a human operator.

The operator reviews the evidence and determines what, if anything, STN-LABZ should do.

That boundary is intentional.

Autonomous systems are useful when they can handle routine work without constantly demanding human attention.

Decisions requiring organizational authority remain human decisions.

## Persistent Intelligence

Rictus performs intelligence operations, but the STN-LABZ website provides a persistent home for the intelligence we choose to retain.

Our design principle is simple:

> **Rictus performs. STN-LABZ preserves.**

That means useful intelligence does not disappear simply because Rictus restarts, receives an update, moves to another system, or is rebuilt.

The intelligence becomes part of the institutional history of STN-LABZ.

## Why We Built Rictus

STN-LABZ develops and operates systems across software, security, distributed computing, and other technical research.

Changes outside our organization can affect what happens inside it.

A vulnerability may appear in software we use.

A project we depend upon may change direction.

New research may solve a problem we are investigating.

An event that seems insignificant today may become important several weeks or months later.

Rictus gives us a way to continuously observe that environment without requiring a person to spend every day trying to read everything.

He watches.

He remembers.

He investigates when necessary.

And when something actually deserves our attention, he tells us.
